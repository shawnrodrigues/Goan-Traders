Information We Collect

When you contact Goan Traders for cement and building materials, we may collect the following personal information to facilitate our services:

  • Your full name and contact details (phone number, email address).
  • Delivery address and project site location.
  • Specific details about your project and material requirements.
  • History of our communications to ensure service continuity.

How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To prepare accurate quotes and process your orders efficiently.
  • To schedule and execute the delivery of cement and building materials to your site.
  • To maintain clear communication regarding your orders, delivery status, and any inquiries.
  • To enhance our overall service quality and customer support.

Information Sharing

We respect your privacy and do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We only share information with third parties under these limited circumstances:

  • With our trusted delivery drivers and logistics partners solely for the purpose of fulfilling your order.
  • If required to comply with a legal obligation, court order, or governmental request.
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of Goan Traders, our customers, or others.

Data Security

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. Your data is stored on secure systems and is only accessible by a limited number of authorized personnel who are required to keep the information confidential.

WhatsApp Communication

When you initiate contact with us via WhatsApp, your communications are governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy. We use this platform strictly for business-related communication, such as order coordination and customer inquiries.

Your Rights

You have control over your personal information. You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask for the correction of any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • Request the deletion of your personal data from our records, subject to legal and contractual obligations.
